What We Do

AWS and AI, mastered equally

We don't try to be everything to everyone. AlgoCubic is built around two deep disciplines — AWS cloud engineering and AI engineering — delivered through staff augmentation or full dev-project delivery, whichever fits how you work.

Core Capabilities
01

AWS & Cloud Engineering

Architecture, migrations, CI/CD, and cost control on AWS. Whether it's standing up infrastructure for a new product or untangling an existing account that's grown past its original design, this is handled by engineers who work in AWS daily.

02

AI Workflow Automation & Agents

Where a workflow is repetitive, rule-bound, or drowning a team in manual steps, we design and build the automation or AI agent that removes it — scoped tightly enough that it actually gets adopted, not shelved.

Ways to Engage
03

Staff Augmentation

You keep ownership of the roadmap and process — we supply engineers who embed directly in your team, attend your standups, and ship inside your existing codebase from week one. Useful when you know exactly what you need built and just need more hands capable of building it.

04

Dev Project Delivery

You bring the brief, we bring the team. AlgoCubic scopes, builds, tests, and ships the product ourselves — from a market-facing idea through to a live, maintained release — so you get one accountable partner instead of assembling a team piece by piece.
