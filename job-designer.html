Web Designer /
UI-UX Engineer

Full-time 📍 Remote

Role Overview

We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented Web Designer who can design modern, high-converting, and user-friendly websites. The ideal candidate should understand both aesthetics and business goals — not just pretty designs, but designs that perform.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design beautiful, high-converting website interfaces and landing pages
  • Create wireframes, prototypes, and interactive mockups in Figma
  • Translate business requirements into user-first design solutions
  • Collaborate closely with developers for pixel-perfect implementation
  • Conduct UI audits and improve existing design systems
  • Maintain consistency across brand guidelines and design components

What We're Looking For

  • Strong portfolio demonstrating modern web design and UI/UX work
  • Proficiency in Figma (or similar tools like Adobe XD)
  • Understanding of HTML/CSS is a strong plus
  • Eye for typography, whitespace, color, and visual hierarchy
  • Ability to think beyond aesthetics — you care about user experience and conversion
  • Fast learner who stays current with design trends and best practices

Bonus Points

  • Experience designing for SaaS products or tech startups
  • Knowledge of motion design or micro-interactions
  • Familiarity with design handoff workflows (Zeplin, Storybook, etc.)
